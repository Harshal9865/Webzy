# Google Flow MCP Transition Video Prompts

These prompts are prepared for generating cinematic opening video clips for the Webzy Royal Wedding Invitation templates.

> **Status**: Google Flow MCP is not currently connected in this environment. Prompts are documented here for manual generation or when the Flow MCP becomes available.

## Clip 1: Royal Red Jharokha (Opening Transition)

- **Template**: `wedding-royal-red-01`
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (Vertical, 1080x1920)
- **Duration**: 6 seconds
- **Output Targets**: `intro.mp4`, `intro.webm` (< 1.5 MB), `poster.jpg`
- **Location Target**: `/public/templates/wedding-royal-red-01/media/`
- **Audio**: Muted / Ambient
- **Text Rule**: Absolutely NO text, names, or lettering in the video (rendered dynamically in code).

### Generation Prompt
```text
Vertical macro shot of a deep burgundy velvet invitation envelope with embossed gold roses and leaves, a blush-pink wax seal at the center where four triangular flaps meet. A finger gently presses the seal, it softly cracks, warm golden light pours from the seam, the four flaps fold outward smoothly and a glowing crimson-and-gold royal arch garden scene is revealed behind. Slow cinematic push-in, shallow depth of field, floating gold dust, no text.
```
